## 1. Programmes for first-years and early undergraduates (spring weeks, insight days, discovery programmes) and 2027 application dates

### Takeaway
London's main entry point for first-years is the paid **spring week / spring insight programme**: 1 to 5 days over Easter (March/April 2027), with applications taken roughly **September 2026 to January 2027** and reviewed on a rolling basis. As of 12 October 2026, J.P. Morgan, UBS, Rothschild & Co, Evercore, Nomura and Jefferies are reportedly open. Morgan Stanley's deadline may be as early as 18 October. Lazard, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, BNP Paribas, Citi, Barclays, Bank of America and PJT are expected to open between October 2026 and January 2027. Most dates come from trackers and are not bank-confirmed.

### Inferences
- A first-year on a 3-year degree should treat **October to November 2026** as the core window. J.P. Morgan (1 Nov), UBS (31 Oct) and possibly Morgan Stanley (18 Oct) close first, and rolling review means applying in the first 1 to 2 weeks after opening matters more than the stated deadline.
- Because J.P. Morgan and others allow one spring application per firm, students must pick a division deliberately (IB vs markets vs other) rather than applying to several.
- Elite boutique spring programmes (Evercore, Rothschild, Lazard, PJT, Piper Sandler) are short (about 3 days) and small. They are still valuable because they reportedly fast-track into the following summer's process.

### Gaps
- Official bank pages could not be opened. No 2027 spring dates are confirmed directly from Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Barclays, Citi, BofA, Deutsche Bank, HSBC or BNP Paribas careers sites.
- No information found on Centerview, Moelis or Perella Weinberg London spring/first-year programmes for 2027.
- No published data on the number of spring week places per bank in London.

## 2. Penultimate-year summer internship (10-week summer analyst) timeline for summer 2027 and 2028, the timeline shift, and the spring-week fast-track route

### Takeaway
London summer 2027 (penultimate-year) recruiting opened around **August to September 2026**. It runs on rolling review, and many windows closed by early or mid October 2026 (BofA 11 Oct, Evercore 11 Oct, Lazard 4 Oct, Moelis 25 Sep). London has moved earlier but still runs well behind the US, where summer 2028 recruiting is already under way. For **summer 2028**, London windows are expected to open around **August to September 2027**, and spring-week fast-track decisions feed directly into that cycle. About 20 to 31% of spring interns convert directly to a summer offer, with a further 38 to 44% fast-tracked into a later stage.

### Inferences
- For a **second-year on a 3-year degree** (penultimate year now), the summer 2027 cycle is late-stage. Remaining London options include Goldman Sachs (rolling), PJT, Peel Hunt (to 4 Dec) and TD Securities (to 25 Oct), plus off-cycle roles.
- For a **first-year on a 3-year degree**, the realistic sequence is: spring week applications (Oct to Dec 2026), then spring week (Mar to Apr 2027), then a fast-track or direct summer 2028 offer, or open applications from about Aug 2027. A spring week effectively lets the student secure a summer 2028 internship about 15 months in advance.
- Direct conversion fell from 31% to 20% between Trackr's 2025 and 2026 surveys, so a spring week should be treated as a strong head start, not a guarantee.

### Gaps
- No bank-confirmed opening dates for London summer 2028. All are projections.
- No authoritative (non-survey) source quantifies how far London timelines have moved year on year. Evidence is anecdotal plus scattered posting dates.
- Trackr's samples are small (232 spring candidates, 296 summer candidates) and self-selected.

## 3. Other routes: off-cycle internships, industrial placement years, graduate schemes, and continental European hubs

### Takeaway
UK students also have 12-month **industrial placements**, applied for in the autumn of second year (UBS, Lazard, J.P. Morgan, Lloyds, BlackRock). They can apply for 3 to 6 month **off-cycle internships** (Barclays, BofA, Rothschild, UBS, Evercore), and a small number of direct **graduate/full-time analyst** roles go mainly to people who didn't intern. Continental Europe (Paris, Frankfurt, Milan, Amsterdam) runs mostly on **off-cycle** internships posted 3 to 6 months before the start date, often 6 months long in France. Local-language fluency is usually required at domestic firms and in Milan.

## 6. Bank tiers in London and what they mean for a candidate

### Takeaway

## 8. Visa and right-to-work considerations for international students (as of 2026)

### Takeaway
International students on a Student visa can generally do spring weeks and summer internships **full-time during official vacations** but are capped at **20 hours/week in term time**. From **1 January 2027** the **Graduate Route shrinks from 24 to 18 months** for bachelor's and master's graduates, with PhD reportedly staying at 36 months (conflicting). Full-time analyst roles need Skilled Worker sponsorship. The general threshold is **£41,700** (since 22 July 2025) and the new-entrant rate is about **£33,400**, so London analyst salaries clear it. Major banks hold sponsor licences, but sponsorship is role-dependent. Some diversity programmes require unrestricted right to work.

### Cited Findings
- Graduate Route: successful applications made **on or after 1 January 2027** get **18 months** (down from 24) for bachelor's and master's graduates. This follows the 14 Oct 2025 Statement of Changes. The application date counts, not the award date, and the route itself is not being removed. — [The PIE News – Shorter Graduate Route from January 2027](https://thepienews.com/shorter-graduate-route-from-january-2027-uk-govt-confirms/); [BUILA](https://www.buila.ac.uk/articles/shorter-graduate-route-from-january-2027-uk-govt-confirms); [University of Surrey help](https://help.surrey.ac.uk/article/how-long-will-i-be-eligible-stay-uk-if-my-application-graduate-route-visa-successful)
- PhD graduates: most sources say the period stays at **36 months**, but one says it drops to 2 years from January 2027. These conflict. — [Scholarship Scanner](https://www.scholarshipscanner.com/blog/uk-graduate-route-shortened-to-18-months-from-january-2027-what-international-students-starting-in-january-2026-need-to-know); [GoStudyIn](https://gostudyin.com/news/uk-graduate-route-visa-2027-changes/)
- Skilled Worker: general minimum **£41,700** or the going rate, whichever is higher, for applications from **22 July 2025** (previously £38,700). The new-entrant rate (under 26, switching from Student/Graduate visa, or in recognised training) is **£33,400** / 70% of going rate, capped at 4 years. — [Jobbatical – Skilled Worker threshold 2026](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/uk-skilled-worker-visa-salary-threshold-employer-guide); [Rowan – New entrant salary](https://withrowan.co.uk/guides/new-entrant-salary); [DavidsonMorris](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/skilled-worker-visa-minimum-salary/)

### Inferences
- Spring weeks (Easter vacation) and 10-week summer internships fall in vacations, so Student visa holders can usually take them. Off-cycle internships during term time may breach the 20-hour cap unless the course formally builds in a placement.
- A London analyst base of about £60k+ comfortably clears the £41,700 general and £33,400 new-entrant thresholds. The practical barrier is the bank's willingness to sponsor that role, not salary.
- Students graduating in 2027 or later will get 18 months on the Graduate Route, so it is a shorter bridge for anyone hoping to switch to sponsorship later.

- The Immigration Skills Charge increase (a cost to sponsoring employers) was not verified in this research.

## 9. Target vs non-target universities, and how non-target students break in

### Takeaway
The commonly cited core UK "targets" are **Oxford, Cambridge, LSE, Imperial, UCL and Warwick**. Common "semi-targets" are **Durham, Bristol, Nottingham, Bath, KCL, Edinburgh, St Andrews and Exeter**. This is practitioner consensus, not bank data. Hiring is broadening at the application level: Goldman's 2026 class came from 500+ universities, and Trackr's spring survey spanned 63 UK universities. For non-target students, the realistic levers are spring weeks, access programmes (SEO, upReach, 10,000 Black Interns), mid-market and boutique firms, off-cycle and industrial placements, and continental-language routes.
