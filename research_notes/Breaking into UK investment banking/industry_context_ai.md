## 1. Deal market 2025-2026: M&A, ECM/IPOs and the London listings debate, DCM/leveraged finance, private equity, league tables, notable deals

### Takeaway
2026 has been a strong but uneven deal year in Europe. H1 2026 deal **value** was the highest since 2021, and EMEA had its best first half in 19 years, while deal **counts** fell. Mega-deals did most of the work. The UK has been the standout target market: foreign buyers and PE have bid for FTSE companies (Schroders, Intertek, Segro, Unilever's food arm). London IPOs stayed thin (7 IPOs raising £577m in H1 2026), and big names such as Wise moved their primary listing to New York. Leveraged finance and PE slowed in Q2-Q3 2026 as a Middle East energy shock pushed inflation and ECB rates up.

### Inferences
- The story for interviews is **"value up, volume down, mega-deals and UK targets"**. Large-cap strategic and PE buyers, often American, are buying UK companies that trade at a discount, while the London IPO window stays narrow. This links the two halves of the "London listings debate": companies leave the market through takeovers (Schroders, Intertek, Segro) and through re-listings (Wise), while few new listings arrive.
- The leaders on the value table (Morgan Stanley, Goldman, Barclays, BofA, JPM) differ from the leaders on the count table (Rothschild, Houlihan Lokey, KPMG, PwC, EY). This shows the split between large-cap bulge-bracket work and mid-market advisory, which matters for Q7: the Big 4 are real M&A advisers by deal count.
- The Q3 2026 PE slowdown and the drop in leveraged-finance issuance since Q1 2026 suggest LBO-driven deal flow and leveraged-finance and sponsor-coverage staffing could be softer into early 2027 unless rates ease. This is an inference from PitchBook and AFME data, not a forecast from those sources.

## 2. Macro context: Bank of England rates, inflation, UK growth, and how rates affect M&A/LBOs

### Takeaway
The BoE cut Bank Rate from 5.25% (Aug 2024) to 3.75% (Dec 2025) and has held it there throughout 2026. In September 2026, three MPC members voted to **hike** to 4%. A 2026 Middle East conflict pushed energy prices up: CPI was 3.1% in Aug 2026 and is forecast to exceed 4% in early 2027. The ECB raised rates in June and September 2026. Higher rates feed directly into LBO financing costs, and PitchBook links the Q3 2026 European PE slowdown to the ECB hikes.

### Inferences
- Mechanism for interviews: higher base rates raise the cost of debt. An LBO's IRR depends on how much debt can be serviced, so higher rates mean either lower leverage (more equity, lower returns) or lower entry prices. This fits the lower PE entry multiples PitchBook reports (8.9x vs 9.8x EV/EBITDA). Rate uncertainty also widens bid-ask spreads between buyers and sellers, which slows exits and helps explain the decade-high exit backlog. Strategic buyers paying with shares (McCormick/Unilever, Anglo/Teck) or cash-rich foreign corporates depend less on leveraged financing.
- The 2026 setting is unusual: UK rates moved from "cutting cycle" to "possible hike". A student should know the current Bank Rate (3.75%), the latest CPI (3.1%, Aug 2026) and the direction of risk (upward), and be ready for a 5 Nov 2026 decision that may change these.

## 4. What the analyst job involves: tasks, divisions, sector vs product groups, hours in London, reforms

### Takeaway
Analysts mostly build financial models in Excel and presentations and pitchbooks in PowerPoint, do research, and turn around edits from seniors. Long hours (commonly 70-90+ per week) and waiting for feedback are the defining features. After the May 2024 death of BofA associate Leo Lukenas, US banks formalised **80-hour caps**: JPMorgan's applies to most cases but exempts live deals, and BofA introduced daily logging. Survey data suggest juniors still averaged about 78 hours in 2025. No London-specific regulatory cap was found.

### Cited Findings
- Core tasks: building financial models in Excel, preparing PowerPoint presentations for client meetings, correcting models and decks, and researching potential clients. A lot of the day is spent waiting for feedback from seniors, so logged hours overstate active work. — [The Forage](https://www.theforage.com/blog/careers/investment-banking-hours); [Vault](https://legacy.vault.com/blogs/in-the-black-vaults-finance-careers-blog/what-do-investment-banking-analysts-really-do-all-day-and-night); [Street of Walls](https://streetofwalls.com/finance-training-courses/investment-banking-overview-and-behavioral-training/investment-banking-analyst/)
- Hours (various vintages):
  - Commonly 70-100 per week. — [The Forage](https://www.theforage.com/blog/careers/investment-banking-hours)
  - HISTORICAL: a 2016 Vault survey found 65% of 1st- and 2nd-year analysts regularly work 70-90 hours. — [Vault](https://legacy.vault.com/blogs/in-the-black-vaults-finance-careers-blog/what-do-investment-banking-analysts-really-do-all-day-and-night)
  - HISTORICAL: a 2021 "working conditions survey" of Goldman first-year analysts reported an average above 95 hours. This was an analyst-produced survey, cited secondhand. — [Haus of Deal](https://www.hausofdeal.com/en/blog/investment-banking-working-hours)
  - A 2025 survey (secondary) says juniors still averaged about 78 hours per week despite caps. — [ibinterviewquestions](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/blog/investment-banking-hours-lifestyle)
- Hours depend heavily on the market, the firm and seniority. — [The Forage](https://www.theforage.com/blog/careers/investment-banking-hours)
- **Reforms (HISTORICAL, 2024, still in force):**
  - JPMorgan capped junior banker hours at **80 per week** "in most cases", with **live deals exempt**. This is on top of an existing "pencils down" window (6pm Friday to noon Saturday) and one guaranteed full weekend off per quarter. — [Fortune, 12 Sep 2024](https://www.fortune.com/2024/09/12/jpmorgan-cap-junior-bankers-hours); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jpmorgan-just-capped-junior-bankers-111004945.html)
  - Bank of America had an 80-hour limit on paper, but a WSJ investigation reported juniors were pressured to under-report hours. BofA introduced daily hour logging for US-based junior bankers, naming deals and supervising seniors. — [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/jpmorgan-bank-of-america-set-80-hour-week-limit-overwork/479789); [HR Grapevine](https://www.hrgrapevine.com/us/content/article/2024-09-19-us-banks-introduce-working-hour-limits-after-tragedy)
  - Leo Lukenas III, 35, a BofA associate, died in May 2024 of a blood clot. The medical examiner did not tie his death to workload. A recruiter said he wanted to leave because of 100-hour weeks. BofA FIG head Gary Howe lost oversight of the FinTech team. — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2024/05/08/leo-lukenas-iii-bank-of-america-junior-investment-banker-death-wall-street-working-conditions); [HR Grapevine](https://www.hrgrapevine.com/us/content/article/2024-10-22-senior-exec-moved-aside-after-bank-of-america-tragedy)
  - HISTORICAL London link: the 2013 death of an intern at Bank of America's London office, after three consecutive all-nighters, prompted the earlier 80-hour policies. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jpmorgan-just-capped-junior-bankers-111004945.html); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/jpmorgan-bank-of-america-set-80-hour-week-limit-overwork/479789)
- 2026 (single blog source): JPMorgan reportedly now checks reported hours against electronic activity records to enforce the 80-hour cap. — [MetaIntro](https://www.metaintro.com/blog/jpmorgan-tech-monitor-junior-banker-hours-2026) (low confidence)

### Inferences
- Divisions a student should be able to define: coverage/M&A (advising on buying and selling companies), ECM (IPOs, follow-ons, block trades), DCM (bond issuance), leveraged finance (debt for LBOs and high-yield issuers), and restructuring (distressed companies). Banks split into **sector (industry) groups**, such as FIG, TMT, healthcare, industrials and natural resources, and **product groups** (M&A, ECM, DCM, LevFin, restructuring). These definitions are general industry knowledge; the searched sources described them only loosely.
- The 2026 market data in Q1 maps onto group-level busyness. M&A coverage teams (especially FIG, given the Schroders, Evelyn and Aegon deals, and consumer) have had a strong 2026. LevFin volumes were down sharply in Q1 2026, and the London ECM window is thin. Restructuring could pick up if rates rise and Fitch's raised default forecast materialises.
- Caps are soft (live deals are exempt) and London has no statutory equivalent. Candidates should expect 70-80+ hour weeks during live deals.

### Gaps
- No London-specific 2025-2026 hours survey and no product-group hours comparison (ECM vs DCM vs LevFin vs restructuring) were found.
- No UK bank (Barclays, HSBC) or European bank hours policy, and no UK regulatory action on junior hours, was found.
- I found no reliable source on whether JPMorgan's or BofA's caps apply to their London offices; the BofA logging rule is explicitly for US-based staff.

## 6. Career paths and exit opportunities; timing of PE recruiting in London

### Takeaway
The standard ladder is analyst, then associate (around years 4-6), then VP, director and MD. The most common exit is to private equity, typically after about 18 months as an analyst. In London, PE recruiting is headhunter-led and less front-loaded than US "on-cycle". Interviews typically run 3-4 months before start dates, in Sept to early Dec and Jan to April windows. US on-cycle for the 2027 class paused in summer 2025 after Dimon threatened to fire analysts who accepted future-dated PE jobs within 18 months, then restarted abruptly in January 2026.

### Cited Findings
- London PE recruiting:
  - IB analysts usually leave for PE after at least one year, most commonly about 18 months.
  - Interviews happen about 3-4 months before the start date, in windows from September to early December and January to April.
  - Headhunters are central, and alumni referrals help.
  - [Mergers & Inquisitions](https://mergersandinquisitions.com/london-private-equity-recruiting)
- US on-cycle (2027 associate class): paused in summer 2025, then restarted suddenly in January 2026, when more than a dozen major funds (Blackstone, Apollo, KKR, Thoma Bravo) ran compressed, short-notice, full-day interviews for jobs starting summer 2027. — [Vault](https://legacy.vault.com/blogs/in-the-black-vaults-finance-careers-blog/private-equity-on-cycle-hiring-is-faster-earlier-and-wilder-than-ever); [Villanova/Vault](https://connections.villanova.edu/blog/2026/01/15/private-equity-on-cycle-hiring-is-faster-earlier-and-wilder-than-ever-vault-article/)
- The pause followed JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon saying the bank would dismiss junior analysts who accepted future-dated PE roles within their first 18 months. — [Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/private-equity-firms-restart-delayed-graduate-hiring-after-recruitment-backlash/); [ibinterviewquestions](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/blog/pe-on-cycle-recruiting-banks-crackdown-2026)
- One analysis expects on-cycle to continue but somewhat later, with more seats moving to off-cycle. This is a forecast. Middle-market and growth funds recruit on a rolling, off-cycle basis. — [ibinterviewquestions](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/blog/pe-on-cycle-recruiting-banks-crackdown-2026); [Leland](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/private-equity-interviews-on-cycle-vs-off-cycle)

- Associates are typically in about years 4-6 of a banking career (guide). — [PrepLounge](https://www.preplounge.com/en/blog/finance/investment-banking/salary/uk)

### Inferences
- Common exits besides PE: hedge funds, corporate development, growth equity, and start-ups or operating roles. These are well known but were not sourced with 2025-2026 data in this round.
- Weak European PE fundraising and a heavy exit backlog may mean fewer London PE associate seats in 2026-27 than in 2021-22. US funds, which are active in Europe, may run more aggressive, earlier processes for London analysts. This is an inference.
- Students should expect pressure to decide on PE within months of starting as an analyst, and should know that some banks (JPMorgan) penalise early acceptance.

### Gaps

- No data was found on what share of London analysts exit to PE versus other routes, or on typical promotion timings (analyst to associate to VP to director to MD) from a primary source.
- Hedge fund, corporate development and growth-equity exit data were not researched.

## 7. Backup and alternative routes into IB

### Takeaway
Big 4 deal advisory is a credible route. KPMG, PwC and EY rank in Europe's top five M&A advisers **by deal count** in H1 2026, and Big 4 lead-advisory or corporate-finance staff (especially after 3 years and the ACA) can move to mid-market banks, then lateral to bulge-bracket or elite boutique firms. Transaction services is more due-diligence and accounting focused and is a weaker bridge. Pre-experience master's degrees (LBS MiF/MFA, LSE MSc Finance, Cambridge MFin) act as a "reset" with strong IB placement: LBS MiF sent 33% of financial-services hires to IB (2022, HISTORICAL).

### Cited Findings
- Big 4 as M&A advisers: KPMG and PwC tied 3rd by European deal count in H1 2026 (44 deals each), with EY 5th (40). KPMG ranked 1st by volume in Q1 2026. PwC was 1st by European deal count in 2025. — [Private Banker International](https://www.privatebankerinternational.com/news/europe-ma-advisers-h1-2026/); [Private Banker International](https://www.privatebankerinternational.com/news/europe-ma-advisers-q1-2026/); [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/408980/europe-financial-advisors-m-a-deal-count/)
- Big 4 to IB route (anecdotal, forum):
  - Big 4 to mid-market IB is "definitely achievable, especially if you stick it out for 3 years and get your ACA".
  - Moves run mainly through headhunters on LinkedIn.
  - Some firms are making internal Audit-to-Deals moves harder.
  - Someone from Big 4 M&A lead advisory might target a mid-market IB associate role, then lateral to a bulge-bracket or elite boutique bank.
  - [Wall Street Oasis](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking/re-big-4-to-ibd-how-does-it-happen-in-the-uk); [Fishbowl](https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/hey-guysi-need-an-advice-from-you-just-finished-mba-from-top-business-school-and-my-goal-is-to-make-a-transition-to-investment)
- Transaction advisory services gives exposure to bankers, but many teams focus on accounting and due diligence rather than valuation and modelling. — [Mergers & Inquisitions](https://www.mergersandinquisitions.com/tag/valuation/) (search summary)
- Flows run both ways: Big 4 M&A teams hired laid-off London bankers (2023-24, HISTORICAL). Deloitte later reviewed and shrank parts of its UK deals unit to focus on larger, sector-focused M&A (date not confirmed). — [City AM](https://cityam.com/?p=1969368); [City AM](https://cityam.com/?p=2122416)
- Boutiques: senior bankers continue to form independent advisory boutiques in London (example article; date not confirmed). Rothschild & Co (an independent adviser) leads European deal count and Houlihan Lokey is 2nd, showing large mid-market and independent advisory franchises. — [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/top-investment-bankers-join-form-boutique-advisory-firm/); [Private Banker International](https://www.privatebankerinternational.com/news/europe-ma-advisers-h1-2026/)

  - LBS Masters in Finance (2022 class, HISTORICAL): 93% of job-seekers had offers within 3 months, and IB was the largest share of financial-services hires at 33%. — [LBS MiF employment report](https://assets.london.edu/hxo16fanegqh/c7089a37-a80b-4175-b344-1914ad1ea176/352ee6ab0277e1a9d2de3a47ab0a7c1f/mif_employment_report.pdf)

  - Comparator, MIT MFin 2025: IBD/transactions advisory 19.6% of accepted roles. — [MIT](https://cdo.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/67/2026/02/2025-MFin-Employment-Report.pdf)
- Graduate hiring criteria are shifting: UBS's 2027 intake requires a 2:1 plus demonstrable AI skills. This is relevant for anyone re-entering via a master's. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ubs-ai-skills-junior-bankers-2027-graduate-hiring)

### Inferences
- Realistic backup ladder for a UK student:
  1. Big 4 lead advisory or M&A, or Corporate Finance, rather than audit or pure transaction services.
  2. A mid-market or boutique bank (Rothschild, Houlihan Lokey, Lazard, and smaller independents).
  3. A lateral move to a bulge-bracket or elite boutique bank once the market is strong.
  
  An alternative is a pre-experience MiF or MSc, with a summer internship during the course as the reset. Corporate development is both an exit and a potential entry route, but it was not sourced here.
- The Big 4's high deal-count rankings make it credible in interviews to describe Big 4 lead advisory as "real M&A work in the mid-market", not just a fallback.

- LSE's IB placement percentage was not found.
- No 2026 data on boutique hiring, off-cycle internships or spring weeks was found. Spring weeks may be covered by other researchers.
- Corporate development as an entry route was not researched.
